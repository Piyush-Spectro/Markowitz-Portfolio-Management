What this run measures

Test period: 2020-12-22 to 2023-11-13. Train window: 252 observations. Rebalance interval: 21 observations. Trading cost: 10 basis points per unit bought or sold. Initial investment is charged. Risk-free assumption: 2.0% annually. Forecast RMSE: 0.002019 in daily-return units.

There were 21 minimum-variance fallbacks where a positive expected excess return was infeasible. All rebalances, costs, forecasts and target weights are included in the adjacent CSV files.

Interpretation and limits

The forecast estimates a holding-period average return. A good forecast score does not guarantee a better allocation. Concentration, covariance estimates and trading costs can outweigh a small predictive advantage. The frontier is a training-sample illustration, not a forecast of realized performance.

The included sample is synthetic. Its results establish that the pipeline runs; they do not establish investment skill. For real-market experiments, use point-in-time adjusted prices and document universe selection, missing observations and corporate actions. This implementation assumes daily observations, close-price execution, full investment, no shorting, proportional costs and no taxes or market impact.

Source and configuration fingerprints are recorded in metadata.json. Educational research; no investment recommendation.
